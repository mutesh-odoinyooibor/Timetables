Teacher accounts

Register teachers in the Learning or Remedial timetable. Each teacher gets one email here automatically. A teacher who appears under similar names in the two timetables still gets only one email. Teachers sign in with that email only, with no password.

So teachers can sign in on their own phones: download published.json, then upload it to GitHub next to timetables.html (Add file → Upload files). Do this each time you add teachers or change a timetable. A teacher sees and downloads only their own timetables.
#TeacherEmailStatus

An account is unnecessary when its teacher is no longer registered in the timetables. To remove a teacher who is still registered, delete them in the Learning timetable first, then clear unnecessary accounts.
